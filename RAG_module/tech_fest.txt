Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India.

He is a professional cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team.

Kohli is widely regarded as one of the best batsmen in the world.

He has scored more than 70 international centuries across formats.

His aggressive batting style and consistency make him a fan favorite.

Kohli played a crucial role in India’s 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup victory.

He has represented Royal Challengers Bangalore in the IPL since 2008.

Fans often call him “King Kohli” for his dominance in cricket.

He married Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in December 2017.

Together, they are considered one of India’s most admired celebrity couples.

Kohli has received awards like the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna and Padma Shri.

He has also been named ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times.

His leadership as captain brought India several memorable Test and ODI wins.

Off the field, he runs a foundation to support underprivileged children.

Kohli’s journey from a young Delhi boy to a global cricket icon reflects determination, discipline, and passion.
